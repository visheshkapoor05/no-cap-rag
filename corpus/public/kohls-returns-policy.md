# Kohl's — Return Policy (summary)

- Standard "Hassle-Free" window: 90 days for most items.
- Kohl's Card holders get an extra 30 days, for 120 days total.
- Shortened windows: premium electronics and watches, 30 days, original
  packaging plus a valid receipt or account lookup required; Sephora at
  Kohl's items, 30 days, new or gently used condition, valid receipt or
  account lookup required.
- Extended window: baby registry items, 365 days, with a gift receipt or
  the original receipt.
- Non-returnable, no exchange either: beverages, opened/unsealed breast
  pumps and their accessories, trading cards.
