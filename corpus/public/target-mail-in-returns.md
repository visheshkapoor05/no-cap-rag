# Target — Mail-In Returns (summary)

- Initiated online through Target.com or the app, under "My Purchases."
- Target emails a free return mailing label and instructions; the item,
  its original packaging, and accessories should all be included in the
  package, then dropped at a UPS or FedEx location.
- Target covers return shipping cost for mail-in returns — the customer
  doesn't pay for the label.
- Refunds can be issued before Target physically receives the returned
  item; if the returned item then fails inspection, the refund can be
  reversed after the fact.
- Damaged, used, or unexpected items generally aren't kept by Target —
  they're donated or recycled; a customer can call to try to recover an
  item sent by mistake, but recovery isn't guaranteed.
- Refunds go to the original payment method in most cases; Target
  GiftCard purchases are refunded as a new eGiftCard instead.
