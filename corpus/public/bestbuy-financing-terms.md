# Best Buy — Storewide Financing / My Best Buy Credit Card (summary)

- The My Best Buy Credit Card offers 0% APR financing for 24 months on
  qualifying purchases.
- This is **deferred-interest** financing, not true 0% financing: minimum
  payments are still required every month during the promo period, and if
  the full purchase amount isn't paid off by the end of the 24 months,
  interest accrues retroactively across the entire promotional period at
  the card's standard APR (reported around 30.74% at time of fetch).
