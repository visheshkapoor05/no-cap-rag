# Nordstrom — Gift Card Info (summary)

- Balance can be checked by phone, at any store register, or online.
  Funds authorized/held against a pending order won't show in the
  displayed balance.
- Gift cards carry no expiration date and no associated fees — an
  unusually customer-favorable term compared to some retailers' gift-card
  policies.
- Redeemable at Nordstrom and Nordstrom Rack stores, Nordstrom.com,
  Nordstromrack.com, the mobile apps, and Nordstrom Local locations. Up to
  ten cards can be applied to a single online order.
- Cards cannot be returned, exchanged for cash (except where legally
  required), resold, or transferred to someone else.
- Lost or stolen cards will not be replaced, and Nordstrom takes no
  responsibility for unauthorized use once a card is lost — customers are
  told to treat the card like cash and to contact customer service
  immediately if it goes missing.
