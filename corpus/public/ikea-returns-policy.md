# IKEA — Return Policy (summary)

- Unopened items: 365 days to return for a full refund.
- Opened items: 180 days to return for a full refund, with proof of
  purchase.
- Required at time of return: photo ID, the receipt or online order
  confirmation, and the original card used for purchase.
- In-store returns use a dedicated "Exchanges & returns" entrance and
  desk, separate from general checkout.
- Online purchases are returned by bringing the item to a physical store's
  returns desk (no general mail-in option described on this page).
- Small items can alternatively be picked up via FedEx at any of 50,000+
  drop-off locations, arranged through customer support.
- Exchanges can be completed at any IKEA store location, not just the
  original purchase location.
- Returns are not accepted at IKEA Planning Studios or standalone Pick Up
  Point locations.
- This page does not spell out category-specific exceptions (e.g.
  mattresses, plants, custom/made-to-order items) — a gap worth flagging
  rather than assuming a rule that isn't actually stated.
