# Best Buy — Return & Exchange Policy (summary)

- Standard members: 15-day return window on most products; "activatable"
  devices (e.g. phones needing carrier activation) get only 14 days.
- My Best Buy Plus / My Best Buy Total members: extended to 60 days on
  most products (Marketplace-sold products excluded); activatable devices
  still capped at 14 days.
- Window starts the day the product is received, not the purchase date.
- Applies across condition types: new, clearance, open-box, refurbished,
  and pre-owned products all fall under the same window rules.
- Items that arrive back damaged, unsanitary, dented, scratched, or
  missing major included contents can have the return denied.
- Returns accepted either in any Best Buy store or via a free prepaid
  FedEx return label.
