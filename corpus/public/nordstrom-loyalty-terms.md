# Nordstrom — The Nordy Club Terms (summary)

- Points accrue on net spend (after returns, discounts, taxes, and
  gift-card redemptions are subtracted) at Nordstrom and Nordstrom Local.
- Base earn rate varies 1–3 points per dollar depending on membership
  tier; the co-branded credit card earns 2 points per dollar on gas,
  grocery, dining, and streaming purchases anywhere the card is accepted,
  not just at Nordstrom.
- Tier thresholds, by annual net purchase balance: Member (no minimum),
  Influencer ($500), Ambassador ($5,000), Icon ($15,000, requires the
  credit card). Status resets each calendar year, with the prior year's
  tier held as a floor.
- Points convert to "Nordstrom Notes" at a fixed rate (roughly 1,000
  points ≈ $10). Notes have no cash value and can't be exchanged for cash
  or credit.
- Notes are redeemable across Nordstrom, Nordstrom Local, Rack, and Last
  Chance, and apply against the full purchase price including fees and
  tax.
- Points expire after 12 consecutive months without program activity.
  Unused annual bonus benefits forfeit at year-end. Cancelling membership
  voids all accumulated points and benefits outright.
