# IKEA — Delivery Terms & Conditions (summary)

- Small-item orders get an estimated delivery date at checkout, but the
  page explicitly notes this estimate is not guaranteed.
- Large-item deliveries are assigned to a delivery provider after the
  order is placed; scheduled deliveries let the customer pick a preferred
  time window.
- Small orders require a $35+ merchandise minimum (before tax/fees/gift
  cards) to qualify for delivery.
- Large-item delivery pricing varies by location, with higher tiered
  pricing in the Northeast and select metro areas.
- Storage fees of up to $10/day apply if a large-item delivery is delayed
  more than 14 days past the purchase date.
- A re-delivery fee applies if the customer isn't available during the
  scheduled delivery window.
- The delivery address cannot be changed after the order is placed — the
  stated reason is that fulfillment starts processing immediately; the
  customer must cancel and reorder instead.
- Large-item deliveries require someone 18+ present to receive them,
  except in select zip codes with an opt-in unattended-delivery program.
- Damage or missing items must be reported within 48 hours of delivery;
  items accepted without noting visible defects at delivery time may not
  be replaceable afterward.
- Pickup orders must be collected within 24 hours of the ready
  notification, or the order is restocked and refunded automatically.
