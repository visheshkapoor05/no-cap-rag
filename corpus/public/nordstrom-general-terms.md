# Nordstrom — General Terms & Conditions (summary)

- Online prices can differ from in-store prices and vary by location;
  Nordstrom reserves the right to correct pricing/description errors
  without prior notice, and disclaims responsibility for typos. Some
  product copy is noted as possibly AI-generated and may contain errors.
- Site content and service are provided "as is," with no warranty of
  accuracy or uninterrupted availability. Liability for losses tied to
  site use or a purchase is capped at the amount paid to Nordstrom in the
  prior 12 months.
- Nordstrom reserves the right to refuse, cancel, or limit any order,
  including capping quantities per account, payment method, or shipping
  address, and refuses sales to resellers/dealers at its discretion.
- Merchandise availability isn't guaranteed even after an order is
  placed; low-stock items can become unavailable.
- Disputes require a 60-day informal resolution attempt first, then
  mandatory arbitration rather than court — a class-action waiver and jury
  trial waiver both apply. Claims must be filed within 12 months or are
  time-barred.
