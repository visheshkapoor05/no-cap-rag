# Walmart — Price Match Policy (summary)

- In-store purchases only match against the identical item's price on
  Walmart.com — Walmart does not match competitor prices at all, which is
  a notably narrower policy than a typical "we match any competitor"
  framing.
- "Identical item" requires matching brand, model, size, color, and
  quantity, and the item must be available both in-store and on
  Walmart.com at the time.
- Third-party Marketplace listings don't qualify, on either side of the
  match (not as the customer's proof, and not as a source Walmart itself
  will match against).
- Walmart.com itself does not price-match: not against its own past
  prices after a price drop, not against competitors, and not against
  Marketplace/third-party sellers.
- Clearance, Rollback, Black Friday, Cyber Monday, and other limited-time
  event pricing are explicitly excluded from matching.
- Limit: one price match per customer per day.
