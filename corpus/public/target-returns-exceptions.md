# Target — Return Policy Exceptions (summary)

- Extended windows: Target Owned Brand items up to 1 year with receipt;
  Target Circle Card holders get roughly 30 extra days on most purchases;
  EBT/SNAP purchases get 90 days (a legal requirement, not a courtesy).
- Shortened windows: non-Apple electronics 30 days; Apple/Beats products
  and mobile phones 14 days; Target Plus (marketplace) partner items
  typically 30 days.
- Final-sale, no return or exchange: trading cards and accessories, opened
  collectibles (dolls, action figures), personalized items, digital
  downloads, opened breast pumps, gift cards of any kind.
- Exchange-only if opened (no refund): music, movies, video games,
  software, airbeds.
- Special handling: Optical, Warby Parker, Pharmacy, Starbucks, and Target
  Tech purchases must be returned/exchanged at their own dedicated area,
  not general Guest Services.
- International purchases cannot be returned in a US store.
- Mobile phone returns can carry a restocking fee up to $35.
- Holiday and limited-run collections may run a different policy than the
  standing rules above.
