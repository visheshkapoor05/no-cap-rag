# Target — Returns Policy (summary)

- Standard window: 90 days from receipt, delivery, or pickup.
- Target Owned Brand items: extended to 1 year with a receipt.
- Target Plus (marketplace) partner items: 30 days from receipt.
- Target Circle Card holders get an extra 30 days layered on top of the
  standard window (roughly 120 days for most items).
- Category carve-outs: non-Apple electronics and entertainment items drop
  to 30 days; Apple/Beats products and mobile phones drop further to 14
  days.
- Several categories are final-sale (no return at all): trading cards and
  accessories, opened/unsealed breast pumps, opened collectibles.
- Music, movies, video games, software, and airbeds are exchange-only once
  opened, not refundable.
- A valid receipt, the return barcode, or the original payment card (for
  lookup) satisfies the receipt requirement; gift receipts refund as a
  Target GiftCard rather than cash/card.
- Refunds return to the original payment method by default; processing
  time varies by payment type (Target Circle Card fastest, third-party
  cards and mail-in returns slower).
