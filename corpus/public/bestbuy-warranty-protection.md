# Best Buy — Product Protection / Total Membership (summary)

- Best Buy Total membership includes up to 24 months of product
  protection on most purchases made while the membership is active.
- Covered mechanical failures are resolved via repair, replacement, or
  store credit; some products also cover accidental damage from handling,
  not just mechanical failure.
- Additional membership benefits bundled in: 24/7 tech support,
  member-only pricing/discounts, free Geek Squad setup on select devices,
  free 2-day shipping on eligible products, expert in-home consultations,
  identity-theft resolution services, and secure data disposal.
- Claims outside the manufacturer's warranty period carry a service fee;
  up to two covered claims are allowed per 12-month period.
- Protection applies only to purchases made from Best Buy directly —
  Best Buy Marketplace products are excluded.
- Cancelling the membership cancels any remaining months of protection
  coverage immediately, not just future new coverage.
