# Gift Card Terms

**Policy ID:** GC-POL-001 · **Version:** 1.0 · **Effective:** January 1, 2026

## 1. Issuance

Gift cards are issued in physical (plastic) and digital (email code) form,
in denominations from $10 to $500. Physical cards are activated at the
point of sale; digital cards are activated on send and are usable
immediately by the recipient. Gift cards purchased with another gift card
are not permitted, to limit money-laundering exposure through repeated
card-to-card conversion.

## 2. Balance and Expiry

Gift card balances do not expire. This is a deliberate difference from
loyalty points (LOY-POL-002), which do expire — the two are legally and
operationally distinct instruments, and this distinction is a common
source of customer confusion worth stating explicitly rather than leaving
implicit. A gift card represents pre-paid store credit; loyalty points
represent an earned, non-purchased reward.

No dormancy or maintenance fee is charged against an unused balance,
regardless of how long the card goes unused.

## 3. Redemption

Gift cards may be redeemed for any in-store or online purchase, applied in
full or in part, in combination with another payment method for any
remaining balance. Gift cards cannot be redeemed for cash except where
required by state law for balances under $10.

Gift cards cannot be used to purchase other gift cards (see Section 1) and
cannot be combined with loyalty points redemption on the same transaction
line — a customer may use points on part of an order and a gift card on a
different part, but not both against the identical line item, to keep
refund-reversal logic on a return unambiguous.

## 4. Loss, Theft, and Fraud

Lost or stolen physical gift cards can be deactivated and the remaining
balance transferred to a new card only if the original purchase receipt or
activation record is available. Without proof of the original balance, no
replacement or transfer is possible — gift cards are treated as bearer
instruments for this purpose, similar to cash.

Digital gift card codes that are forwarded, resold, or otherwise transfer
outside the original purchaser's control are the purchaser's
responsibility; the program does not investigate or reverse redemptions by
a third party in possession of a valid, unredeemed code.

## 5. Returns Involving Gift Cards

If an item purchased with a gift card is returned, the refund is issued
back to a gift card, not to a different payment method, regardless of
whether the original gift card is still active. This applies even if the
original card has since been fully spent down or deactivated — a new gift
card is issued for the refund amount in that case.
