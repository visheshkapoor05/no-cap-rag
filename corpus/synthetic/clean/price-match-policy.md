# Price Match Policy

**Policy ID:** PM-POL-001 · **Version:** 1.0 · **Effective:** February 1, 2026

## 1. Eligibility

A price match is available when an identical item — same brand, model,
size, and color — is found at a lower price at a named competitor,
verified at the time of purchase or within 14 days after, with proof (a
current ad, printed page, or the competitor's live product page). Auction
sites, marketplace third-party sellers, and membership-club-exclusive
pricing are not eligible competitor sources.

## 2. What Is Not a Price Match

A promotional discount under PROMO-POL-002 is not a price match — this
distinction matters specifically because RET-POL-001's shortened return
window for discounted items (Section 3.2) does not apply to a price-matched
item, per PROMO-POL-002 Section 1. A price match restores parity with a
competitor's price; it does not create a new promotional discount.

Flash sales, doorbuster events, and one-day-only competitor pricing are not
eligible for matching, since these are explicitly time-boxed and not
representative of the competitor's standing price.

## 3. Process

The match is applied at checkout for in-store purchases, or via a
post-purchase price-match request within the 14-day window for online
purchases. A store associate verifies the competitor price against the
proof provided; associates have discretion to decline a match if the
competitor listing appears to be a pricing error (a price implausibly far
below cost) rather than a genuine standing price.

For a post-purchase request submitted online, the customer uploads proof
through the order-history page rather than contacting support directly —
requests submitted by phone or email instead are redirected to the online
form, which has added an average of 1–2 days to resolution time for those
customers historically, worth mentioning proactively when a customer calls
in instead of using the form. The system automatically checks the
submitted competitor URL against a list of pre-approved competitor domains
before a human associate ever reviews the request; a URL from a domain not
on that list is auto-rejected regardless of how legitimate the listed
price appears, and the customer is directed to resubmit with a different
qualifying source rather than having a human override the domain check at
this stage. Associates reviewing a request that passed the automatic
domain check still independently re-verify the price itself, since the
domain check only confirms the source is eligible, not that the specific
price shown is currently accurate — competitor prices captured by
screenshot can be stale by the time the request reaches manual review,
particularly for fast-moving promotional pricing at the competitor's end.

## 4. Repeated or Suspicious Requests

A customer submitting more than 5 price-match requests within a rolling
30-day period is flagged for manual review before any further request is
processed automatically, regardless of whether each individual request
would otherwise qualify under Sections 1–3 above. This is not a blanket
denial — legitimate frequent shoppers do submit this many matches — but
the pattern is also consistent with price-match abuse using fabricated or
manipulated competitor listings, and manual review is the only reliable
way to distinguish the two cases from request volume alone.

## 5. Refund of the Difference

For a price match applied after purchase, the difference is refunded to
the original payment method within 5 business days, not issued as store
credit or points. If the original purchase used loyalty points in whole or
in part, the price-match refund is issued in cash/card only — it does not
adjust or return points, since points were spent at the original,
unmatched price and the transaction underlying that spend is not being
reversed, only partially refunded.

## 6. One Match Per Item

Only one price match is honored per item per transaction, even if multiple
qualifying competitor prices are found. If a customer later finds a still
lower price after a match has already been applied, a second match against
the same item is not permitted — the customer's recourse at that point is
the standard return window under RET-POL-001, followed by a new purchase
at the lower price if still available.
