# Shift Policy

**Policy ID:** STAFF-POL-001 · **Effective:** January 1, 2026

## 1. Scheduling

Schedules are posted 14 days in advance. Shift-swap requests between
employees are permitted with shift-lead approval, provided both employees
are qualified for the role being covered (e.g., only keyholders may cover
opening/closing shifts, per SOP-OPEN-002's individual-code requirement in
Section 1.1 and 4.5).

A posted schedule can be changed by store management with less than 14
days' notice only in a defined emergency scenario — a same-day no-show
under Section 5 below, a documented staffing shortfall from approved leave
under STAFF-POL-003, or a store-wide event requiring additional coverage
announced with as much notice as practical. A schedule change made outside
one of these scenarios requires the employee's affirmative agreement, not
just notification — an employee can decline a non-emergency schedule
change without it being treated as a scheduling-compliance issue, though
declining repeatedly may factor into future shift-swap approval priority
at the shift lead's discretion.

Availability changes (an employee's standing days/hours they can work, as
distinct from a one-off swap) must be submitted at least 7 days before the
next schedule posting to be reflected in that posting; changes submitted
after that cutoff are reflected starting the following schedule cycle
instead, not retroactively inserted into an already-posted schedule, since
retroactive availability changes have historically caused cascading
conflicts with other employees' already-confirmed shifts.

## 2. Breaks

Shifts of 5 hours or more include one paid 15-minute break and one unpaid
30-minute meal break. Shifts under 5 hours include one paid 15-minute break
only. Breaks must be taken; an employee cannot decline a break in exchange
for leaving early, since break compliance is a labor-law requirement, not a
scheduling convenience.

## 3. Overtime

Overtime (hours beyond 40 in a work week) requires shift-lead
pre-approval except in a genuine emergency — an unplanned register or
till issue mid-close, for instance. Retroactive overtime approval is
granted only when the employee can point to a specific documented cause,
such as a till discrepancy investigation under SOP-CASH-009 Section 5 that
ran past scheduled shift end.

## 4. Minimum Rest Between Shifts

A minimum of 10 hours must elapse between the end of one shift and the
start of the next, including for closing-to-opening ("clopening")
schedules. A closing shift ending at 10 PM cannot be followed by an opening
shift starting before 8 AM the next day.

## 5. Attendance and No-Shows

Two unexcused no-shows within a rolling 90-day period trigger a formal
attendance review with the store manager. A no-show is distinct from a
late arrival — arriving late is handled informally by the shift lead unless
it becomes a pattern, but a full no-show with no contact before shift start
is always logged formally regardless of the reason given afterward.
