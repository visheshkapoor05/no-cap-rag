# Returns Policy

**Policy ID:** RET-POL-001 · **Version:** 2.0 · **Effective:** June 1, 2025
**Superseded by:** Returns Policy v3.0, effective June 1, 2026

## 1. Standard Return Window

Most unopened items in new condition may be returned within **30 days** of
the delivery or in-store purchase date for a full refund to the original
payment method. This window applies uniformly across product categories
unless a specific exception is listed in Section 3.

Proof of purchase is required for all returns. A receipt, packing slip, or
order confirmation email is accepted. Returns without proof of purchase are
handled under the store's discretion policy, at a rate not to exceed store
credit for the item's lowest recorded selling price in the prior 90 days.

## 2. Non-Returnable Items

The following categories are final sale and cannot be returned under this
policy, regardless of condition: gift cards, personalized or
custom-engraved items, perishable goods, and any item marked "Final Sale"
at the point of purchase. Store associates are not authorized to override
this list; exceptions require regional manager sign-off, logged per
SOP-RET-014.

## 3. Exceptions

### 3.1 Electronics

Electronics and entertainment items must be returned within **15 days** of
purchase, unopened, in original packaging with all accessories included. A
restocking fee of up to 15% applies to opened electronics accepted at
associate discretion outside this window.

### 3.2 Discounted Items

Items purchased at a markdown or promotional discount of 20% or more may be
returned within **30 days** of purchase, the same as the standard window —
discounted items receive no separate, shortened window under this version
of the policy. This is called out explicitly here because it is the
specific provision that changes under the next policy revision, and because
store associates have historically applied the electronics window (Section
3.1) to discounted electronics in error; the discount exception and the
electronics exception are independent and do not stack to shorten a window
further than either alone would.

### 3.3 Items Purchased Partly or Fully with Loyalty Points

Returns of items purchased using loyalty points, in whole or in part, are
permitted under the same windows described above. How the refunded value is
returned to the customer is governed by the Loyalty Program Rules
(LOY-POL-001), not this policy.

## 4. Refund Method

Refunds are issued to the original payment method where possible. Cash
purchases over $75 are refunded as store credit rather than cash, to reduce
till discrepancy risk at close. Refunds involving loyalty points are
processed per LOY-POL-001.
