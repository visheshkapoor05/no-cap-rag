# Leave Policy

**Policy ID:** STAFF-POL-003 · **Effective:** January 1, 2026

## 1. Leave Types

Four leave types are recognized: paid time off (PTO), sick leave, bereavement
leave, and unpaid personal leave. Each accrues and is requested separately;
they are not interchangeable, and unused sick leave cannot be converted to
PTO at year end or on separation.

## 2. PTO Accrual

PTO accrues at 1.25 days per month of full-time employment, capped at 15
days banked at any time. Part-time employees accrue on a pro-rated basis
by scheduled hours. PTO requests require 7 days' advance notice except
where Section 5 (emergency leave) applies.

## 3. Sick Leave

Sick leave accrues at 1 day per month, capped at 6 days banked. Unlike PTO,
sick leave may be used with no advance notice — a same-day call-out is
treated as a valid sick-leave use, not an unexcused no-shift under
STAFF-POL-001 Section 5, provided it is reported before the scheduled
shift start.

## 4. Bereavement Leave

Up to 3 paid days are granted for the death of an immediate family member
(spouse, parent, child, sibling), separate from and not deducted against
PTO or sick leave balances. Extended bereavement beyond 3 days draws
against PTO if available, or unpaid personal leave (Section 6) if not.

## 5. Emergency Leave Exception

The 7-day advance notice requirement in Section 2 is waived for a genuine
emergency — a family medical emergency, a home emergency requiring
immediate presence, or a comparable unforeseeable event. Emergency PTO use
still draws against the employee's existing PTO balance; it is an
exception to the notice requirement only, not an additional leave
allowance beyond what Section 2 already grants.

## 6. Unpaid Personal Leave

Once PTO and applicable sick/bereavement balances are exhausted, an
employee may request unpaid personal leave, subject to store manager
approval and store staffing needs at the time of request. Unpaid leave
beyond 5 consecutive days requires regional HR approval, not just the
store manager.

## 7. Leave and Loyalty Program Interaction

An employee on extended unpaid leave (Section 6) beyond 60 days has their
personal loyalty account (if enrolled, per LOY-POL-001) treated as any
other inactive account under LOY-POL-002 Section 3 — employment status
does not exempt an account from the standard 24-month inactivity rule, and
extended leave alone does not reset that clock.
