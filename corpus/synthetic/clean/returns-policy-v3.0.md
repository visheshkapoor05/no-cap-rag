# Returns Policy

**Policy ID:** RET-POL-001 · **Version:** 3.0 · **Effective:** June 1, 2026
**Supersedes:** Returns Policy v2.0 (effective through May 31, 2026)

## 1. Standard Return Window

Most unopened items in new condition may be returned within **30 days** of
the delivery or in-store purchase date for a full refund to the original
payment method.

## 2. Non-Returnable Items

The following are final sale and cannot be returned under this policy:
gift cards, personalized or custom-engraved items, perishable goods, and
items marked "Final Sale" at the time of purchase.

## 3. Exceptions

### 3.1 Electronics

Electronics and entertainment items must be returned within **15 days** of
purchase, unopened, in original packaging.

### 3.2 Discounted Items

Items purchased at a markdown or promotional discount of 20% or more must be
returned within **14 days** of purchase. This is a change from the 30-day
window under Returns Policy v2.0 — the shorter window was introduced to
reduce return-driven margin loss on clearance-priced inventory.

This exception does not apply to items purchased during standard,
non-promotional pricing, even if a coupon was applied at checkout.

### 3.3 Items Purchased Partly or Fully with Loyalty Points

Returns of items purchased using loyalty points, in whole or in part, are
permitted under the same windows described in Sections 1–3.2 above. However,
**how the refunded value is returned to the customer** — as points, as
original payment method, or a blend of both — is governed by the Loyalty
Program Rules, not this policy. See **LOY-POL-001, Section 4 (Points Handling
on Returns)** for the mechanism.

## 4. Refund Method

Refunds are issued to the original payment method where possible. Cash
purchases are refunded as store credit above $75. Refunds involving loyalty
points are processed per LOY-POL-001 Section 4.
